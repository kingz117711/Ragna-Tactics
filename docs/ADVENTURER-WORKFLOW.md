# Independent adventurer play

## Design references

Ragnarok Online has separate equipment, inventory, status and skill shortcuts. Origin describes one manual skill bar and a separately configured auto-combat selection, with progression through Novice training and job advancement. Ragna Tactics applies those player workflows independently to each member of the roster.

## Play it

1. Choose one adventurer. Use Character in the main bar to open the non-modal equipment, skills, stats and advancement workspace. Equip a compatible item from the shared bag and inspect its effect first. First-job advancement into a core class adds that class’s starter weapon to shared storage. At camp, insert loose cards in eligible Renewal gear and remove them back into storage. Cards follow the equipment instance. Refining failures that break gear return its socketed cards.
2. Open Skills at camp. Six core first-job lines each have two extra techniques, including descendant jobs. Learn ranks at the shown Job levels, assign them to keys 2 and 3, then choose whether each can auto-cast. Key 1 runs the class signature. The overall automatic skill setting controls all automatic casts. Timers and slots belong to the adventurer and persist on reload.
3. Select a single hero on the current battlefield or from the roster. Use the field console to support a living party member nearby, focus a living boss or switch to solo farming. Support follows the chosen ally and prioritizes its target and healing needs. Solo detaches the unit from its party after combat and uses the current field. Deploy them separately from the command deck when ready. Click hotbar skills or use keys 1–3 on that field. Selecting one character also scopes Move, Hold, Focus, Retreat, Farm and Hunt Area to that unit. Select several for group commands.
4. Keep other adventurers farming or fighting on their own fields. Opening a workspace or issuing an individual skill does not pause their simulation.

## Scope and checks

This adds twelve field techniques with distinct effects in addition to the existing signature skills and builds. It uses rank gates and independent cooldowns. Legacy saves acquire the controls through optional fields. Map authoring and PR #8 remain independent. Class outfits and skill effects use the existing resource set.
